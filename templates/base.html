Municipality of Maramag • Province of Bukidnon

Fair, Multi-Criteria Relief Allocation for Maramag

SmartAid resolves the challenge of distributing limited emergency relief and localized social welfare across the 20 barangays of Maramag, Bukidnon. Powered by mathematical Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA), income eligibility screening, and tamper-proof QR disbursement vouchers.

1. Hard Eligibility Screening

Deterministic income ceiling gating (₱15,000 threshold) and deduplication cooldown filters immediately prevent ineligible and duplicate claims before ranking.

2. Vector MCDA & Constrained Allocation

Calculates a normalized Vulnerability Priority Index (VPI $\in [0, 1]$) across poverty, dependency, calamity, and housing, capped strictly to available program slots.

3. Cryptographic QR Disbursement

Approved beneficiaries receive tamper-proof SHA-256 signed QR vouchers instantly verifiable by field agents with one-click double-claim prevention.
