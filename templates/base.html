Transparent, Multi-Criteria Relief Allocation

SmartAid resolves the challenge of distributing limited emergency relief and social assistance when demand far exceeds inventory. Powered by mathematical Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA), income eligibility screening, and tamper-proof QR disbursement vouchers.

1. Hard Eligibility Screening

Deterministic income ceiling gating and deduplication cooldown filters immediately prevent ineligible and duplicate claims before ranking.

2. Vector MCDA & Constrained Allocation

3. Cryptographic QR Disbursement
